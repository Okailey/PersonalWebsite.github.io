About Me

Hi! I'm Danielle Quaye, a recent graduate from Mount Holyoke College with a degree in Computer Science and have a passion for creating/contributing to innovative solutions that help others. My interests lie in frontend development, product management, artificial intelligence, and robotics. Originally from Ghana, I bring a unique perspective and dedication to my work, aiming to build technology that can make a real impact in society.

I have hands-on experience developing web applications, software design, and I'm always eager to learn new tools and techniques to expand my skills. I explored motion planning with Duckiebots, which deepened my interest in robotics and autonomous systems. This experience taught me to solve complex challenges creatively, blending technical knowledge with problem-solving skills. Recently, I have started learning more about cloud infrastructure and deployment.

There have been many exciting projects I have worked on, and you can view them here. Through these projects I gained experience with Python, Flask, HTML/CSS, JavaScript, Java, Swift, TypeScript, Cloud Infrastructure, React.js, Node.js and databases.

I love this path because I find joy in seeing a positive impact from my work. Whether it's contributing to solving major challenges in the lives of individuals or communities, I am driven by the potential of technology to create meaningful change. I really believe that empathy is just as important as my technical skills. My emotional intelligence allows me to adapt to diverse teams, understand user needs, and create technology that truly makes a difference.

Outside of coding, I enjoy playing tennis and volunteering with Dennis Tennis Serving Love Nonprofit, mentoring children interested in tennis.
